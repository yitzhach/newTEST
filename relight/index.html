Source

Measured normals and true albedo from N exposures with the light moved. Fitting ambient needs the lights to differ in elevation — at one elevation the system is singular and the solve is refused. Shoot at least one more exposure than there are unknowns (3 without the ambient term, 4 with it): with nothing spare the model fits perfectly by construction and the Fit view cannot tell you anything.

Surface recovery

Azimuth is the direction the original photograph was lit from. Only the slope along it is recoverable from one image; perpendicular is unconstrained.
