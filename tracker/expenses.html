Import sales CSV

A Square or Stripe export, downloaded as CSV. The file is read here on your device and nothing is uploaded. Rows that cannot be read are refused and listed rather than guessed at, refunds are skipped, and a row with no readable amount comes in unpriced instead of as $0.

The export knows a date, not which weekend it was. This is your call, not the file’s.
