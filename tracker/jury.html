How it will work

  1. You put together five works and a booth shot, and say what you want looked at.
  2. A juror claims it. Nothing is owed until somebody claims it — you are never charged for work that has not started.
  3. They score it and write back. You keep the feedback.

There is no payment anywhere in this app yet, so nothing can be bought and nothing has been charged.
